\begin{abstract}
A textbook on K3 surfaces built from the arithmetic of integral lattices upward,
written for a reader who knows linear algebra, a first course in complex
analysis, and the vocabulary of algebraic geometry, and who wants to reach the
frontier where the transcendental lattice of a K3 surface, the monodromy of a
Picard--Fuchs equation, and the geometry of a modular curve meet. Standard
theorems are stated with references and marked \Lstatus; exact computations that
the reader can rerun are marked \Estatus; a small core of lattice facts that
have been checked by the Lean~4 kernel in a public repository is marked
\Kstatus, and the last chapter explains what that mark does and does not mean.
No physics is discussed.
\end{abstract}
